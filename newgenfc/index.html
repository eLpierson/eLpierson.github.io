The Method

How We Develop Players Differently

01

Technical Foundation

Every session starts with ball mastery and first-touch work inspired by Dutch and Spanish academy models. We obsess over technique because it unlocks everything else.

02

Tactical Intelligence

We teach players to read the game — spatial awareness, decision-making under pressure, and positional play. Smart players make fast players look slow.

03

Creative Freedom

We never coach creativity out of a player. Skills, flair, and improvisation are celebrated — because the best players in the world play with joy and expression.

04

Physical & Mental Resilience

Age-appropriate conditioning, agility work, and mental performance training build athletes who are strong, fast, and confident under pressure.

05

Video Analysis & Feedback

Players review their own footage with Coach Jason to identify patterns, celebrate growth, and set concrete goals. Data-driven development, not guesswork.

06

Character Development

Discipline, leadership, sportsmanship, and accountability. We develop people first and players second — because character is the ultimate competitive advantage.

Meet the Coach

Jason Leiva

Founder & Head Coach

Born and raised in San Francisco, Jason's love for the beautiful game started on the streets and fields of the city. He developed his skills through the SF Glens organization, competing at the MLS Next level as an attacking and defensive midfielder — learning to see the game from every angle.

Now a semi-professional player, Jason combines his active playing career with a deep passion for coaching the next generation. He has studied the training methodologies of Europe's top academies and brings that knowledge home to San Francisco — because he believes every kid in this city deserves access to world-class development, not just the ones who can afford to travel for it.

Jason founded SF New Gen Soccer Academy with one mission: to build the most technically advanced, tactically intelligent, and mentally tough young players San Francisco has ever produced.

FAQ

Common Questions

What age groups do you train?

We train players ages 4 through 18, with programs specifically designed for each developmental stage: Little Ballers (4-6), Development Academy (7-12), and Elite Performance (13-18). Private 1-on-1 sessions are available for all ages.

Where do training sessions take place?

We train at various fields and facilities across San Francisco. Exact locations are shared upon enrollment and may vary by program and season. We also offer private sessions at a location convenient for you.

Does my child need prior soccer experience?

Not at all! Our Little Ballers program is designed for complete beginners. For older beginners, we'll assess your child's level and place them in the right group to ensure they're challenged but not overwhelmed.

How is New Gen different from a regular soccer club?

Most clubs focus on games and winning. We focus on player development using European academy methods — small groups, high repetitions, technical mastery, tactical intelligence, and individualized feedback. We develop players, not just teams.

Can my child do New Gen and their club team?

Absolutely. Many of our players train with us in addition to their club or rec league. Our training complements and accelerates what they're learning elsewhere. In fact, it's one of the most popular ways families use our programs.

Do you offer free trials?

Yes! We offer a complimentary trial session so your child can experience the New Gen difference firsthand. Contact us to schedule one.

What is the coach-to-player ratio?

We maintain a maximum 6:1 player-to-coach ratio in group sessions. This ensures every player gets meaningful individual attention, feedback, and repetitions — something you won't find at larger academies.
